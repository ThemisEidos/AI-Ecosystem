## When to use
Use this skill when you need to perform browser research on example domains or similar placeholder websites to understand their purpose and content.

## Procedure
1. Open your web browser and navigate to the desired example domain (e.g., https://example.com).
2. Observe the content displayed on the page. For example, the page may indicate that it is an example domain for documentation purposes.
3. Take note of any specific messages or disclaimers provided on the site, such as instructions to avoid using the domain in operations.
4. If applicable, look for links or additional resources that may provide further information about the domain's intended use.
